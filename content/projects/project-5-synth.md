### Overview

Create a 2–3 minute original music composition using **Vital**, a powerful wavetable synthesizer. You’ll design your own synth patches and use them to compose in any genre that inspires you—ambient, electronic, hip-hop, synthwave, cinematic, or otherwise. This project focuses on exploring sound design and synthesis as creative tools within your chosen musical style.

---

### Learning Goals

* Understand the core principles of sound synthesis (oscillators, filters, modulation, and envelopes).
* Create and manipulate original synth patches in Vital.
* Apply synthesis and compositional techniques that reflect a chosen genre.

---

### Project Components

1. **Synth Patch Portfolio**
   * Design **3–5 unique synth patches** in Vital.
   * Each patch should serve a role (bass, pad, lead, effect, etc.) and reflect genre-specific aesthetics.
   * Label each patch clearly (e.g., “Dreamy Pad – Ambient,” “Dark Bass – Hip-Hop”).
2. **Composition (2–3 minutes)**
   * Compose a short piece that uses your patches as the primary sound sources.
   * Focus on structure, texture, and how your sound design supports your chosen genre.
3. **Reflection**
   * Write a short reflection describing your creative process:
     * How you approached designing each patch.
     * How synthesis shaped the style and mood of your piece.
     * Any challenges or discoveries along the way.

---

### Submission Checklist

* 3–5 Vital preset files (file type: .vital).
* A 2–3 minute composition (Reaper project file and exported WAV).
* Written reflection (roughly 250–400 words).
